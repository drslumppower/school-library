# Revision sheet - Holiday Survey

## The task

Interview classmates about their summer holidays, collect details and write complete sentences in the **preterit**.

## Preterit forms to know

| Base form | Preterit | Type |
|---|---|---|
| watch | watched | regular |
| cook | cooked | regular |
| practise | practised | regular |
| visit | visited | regular |
| go | went | irregular |
| have | had | irregular |
| break | broke | irregular |
| read | read `/red/` | irregular |

## Build a sentence

### Affirmative

> Subject + verb in the preterit + complement

- She **went** to the beach.
- He **watched** a good movie.
- They **visited** their grandparents.

### Question

> Question word + did + subject + base verb + complement?

- Where **did** you **go**?
- What **did** you **cook**?
- Which book **did** you **read**?
- Who **did** you **visit**?

### Short answer

- Did you go abroad? — **Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.**
- Did you practise sports? — **Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.**

## Questions for details

- **Which country** did you visit?
- **What** did you watch?
- **What happened?**
- **Which beach** did you go to?
- **How old** did you turn?
- **Which sport** did you practise?
- **Which family members** did you visit?

## Checklist

- ai-je utilisé le prétérit pour une action terminée ?
- ai-je ajouté **-ed** aux verbes réguliers ?
- ai-je vérifié les verbes irréguliers ?
- ai-je utilisé la base verbale après **did** ?
- ai-je donné au moins un détail précis ?
