# Quiz

Choose or write the correct answer, then open the correction.

## Question 1

Complete: “I ___ fifteen years old.”

??? success "See the answer"
    **I am fifteen years old.** English uses *be*, not *have*, to express age.

## Question 2

Correct this sentence: “I have two cat.”

??? success "See the answer"
    **I have two cats.** A noun is plural after *two*.

## Question 3

Which sentence naturally describes a regular activity: “I do swim” or “I swim”?

??? success "See the answer"
    **I swim.** You can also say **I go swimming**.

## Question 4

Translate: « J'ai une sœur. »

??? success "See the answer"
    **I have a sister.**

## Question 5

Complete with the correct form: “My parents ___ from Italy.”

??? success "See the answer"
    **My parents are from Italy.**

## Question 6

Which question word asks about a place?

??? success "See the answer"
    **Where?**

## Question 7

Ask a question about the number of pets someone has.

??? success "See the answer"
    **How many pets do you have?**

## Question 8

Translate: « Je pense que la deuxième phrase est fausse. »

??? success "See the answer"
    **I think the second statement is a lie.**

## Question 9

Complete: “I ___ play the piano.” Use a modal verb to express ability.

??? success "See the answer"
    **I can play the piano.** A modal verb is followed by the base form of the verb.

## Question 10

Correct this sentence: “I likes swimming.”

??? success "See the answer"
    **I like swimming.** The verb does not take *-s* after *I*.

## Question 11

Write a follow-up question for the statement “I went to Canada last summer.”

??? success "See the answer"
    Possible answers include **Who did you go with?**, **Where did you stay?**, or **How long did you stay?**

## Question 12

How many true and false statements are needed for the activity?

??? success "See the answer"
    Three true statements and two false statements.

## Self-assessment

- **10 to 12 correct answers**: you are ready for the activity.
- **7 to 9 correct answers**: review *be*, *have* and question words.
- **0 to 6 correct answers**: reread the lesson and correct the model sentences again.
